01 About

Curtis Darst is a Senior Solutions Architect in AWS Worldwide Public Sector, where he owns the technical relationship for a portfolio of strategic EdTech ISVs worth $38.5M in annual recurring revenue. Their products — learning management, student information systems, assessment platforms, enrollment management, and adaptive courseware — reach K-12 districts, universities, and state education agencies.

His work runs from whiteboard to production: generative AI and agentic architectures on Amazon Bedrock, on-premises to cloud migrations, serverless modernization, and the data foundations that make ML workloads possible. Before the strategic accounts role he was lead technical advisor to research universities and liberal arts colleges across a Great Lakes and NJ/PA territory.

Before AWS, he built statewide K-12 data, assessment, and federal reporting systems for the West Virginia Department of Education, directed engineering and server infrastructure at a market research firm, and taught mathematics as an adjunct instructor. He is a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science researching how prompt engineering patterns affect the quality of LLM-generated code, and lives in Powell, Ohio, just north of The Ohio State University campus.

ModernBERT Inference Benchmarking
Open source · MIT-0

Exports one model to four formats and deploys it across nine SageMaker AI configurations — CPU, GPU, Inferentia2, and Serverless — measuring P50/P95/P99 latency, throughput under concurrency, and cold start, then reporting cost per million inferences. Cost varies by more than 40× across configurations serving the identical model. Includes automated teardown with verified deletion, a runtime cost gate, and 166 unit tests.
